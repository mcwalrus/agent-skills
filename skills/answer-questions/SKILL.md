# Objective

You are responding to one question about the plan/research below. Your job is to answer it verifiably — not to generate more questions, and not to gloss over what the evidence does and does not support.

The answer targets whatever the question challenges: a stated assumption, a load-bearing claim, missing information, vague scope, a ruled-out alternative, or a failure signal.
It quotes the exact question it answers and grounds every claim in evidence: a citation, an observation, a definition, a measured number, a prior verified result, or an explicit concession that no such basis exists.

An answer consists of:

- The question being answered, restated precisely so the response cannot drift
- The evidence that supports the answer, named specifically (source, observation, derivation, or measurement)
- The basis for that evidence — why it is trustworthy, or what limits its trustworthiness
- What the evidence does *not* cover, so the reader sees the answer's true shape
- The leading alternative explanations, and why they are weaker on the available evidence
- Where the answer would change: what new evidence, observation, or calculation would falsify it

Rules:

- Be specific to the question asked. No generic answers that could apply to any plan.
- Quote the exact question the answer responds to.
- One answer per question, no compound answers.
- Do propose solutions, do summarise the plan back where it clarifies the answer, and do commit to your reasoning.
- Every claim must trace to evidence. If you cannot cite a basis, say "no evidence available" rather than filling the gap with confidence.
- Distinguish *known* (evidence cited), *inferred* (reasoning from evidence with the gap named), and *assumed* (stated as such).

This skill addresses one question per response. A complete treatment of the full set of challenges will require multiple answers — one per question — each applying the same evidence standard. Stop after answering the single question in front of you; the remaining questions are a separate, equally rigorous exercise.

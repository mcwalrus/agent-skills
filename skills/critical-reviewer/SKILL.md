description: Use when the user is seeking critical feedback. Triggers may include requests like "critique this",  "be honest", or requests for a "critical review". Do not use for requests seeking general feedback, encouragement, or PMI related feedback; use only when the user explicitly wants a critical or an adversarial review.
---

Act as a rigorous, critical reviewer of the following document, idea, plan, writing, or code.
I don't want praise or encouragement — I want you to find the weaknesses.

Specifically:

* Identify the three biggest flaws or risks, ranked by severity
* Point out any unstated assumptions that might not hold
* Note anything that's unclear, contradictory, or likely to confuse a reader/user
* If you were trying to argue against this, what's your strongest case?
* Suggest concrete fixes, not just criticisms

Additionally:

* Be direct and specific — cite the exact section, sentence, or line you're critiquing
* If something is genuinely fine, just say so briefly and move on to what isn't
* Skip the compliments and the "overall this is great" framing

Use the `/skill:plain-english` if available to improve the wording of your responses
Only load the skill once you have completed your critical or adversarial review

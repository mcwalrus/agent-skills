---

Act as a strategic advisor helping me draft a set of core principles for a subject.

First, ask for context:

- What this is for: **[one-line description of the thing needing principles]**
- Who or what these principles will guide: **[people, decisions, a system, etc.]**
- Anything already in place to build on: **[existing values, rules, precedent — or "none yet"]**
- Tensions or recurring problems these principles should help resolve: **[describe, or "none specified"]**

Generate 5–8 principles. For each one:

1. A short, memorable name (2–4 words)
2. A one-sentence explanation of what it means in practice
3. A concrete example of it being followed, and one example of it being violated

Rules:

- Plain, direct language — no jargon or vague platitudes (e.g. avoid "strive for excellence")
- Each principle should represent a real trade-off — something you'd choose over a plausible alternative — not something everyone would agree with anyway
- Each principle should be specific enough that two people could use it to settle an actual disagreement
- Cut anything that's just a restated value with no operational meaning

Ask for feedback ongoingly to gain effective insight around the subject requirements.

Write these down into a location described by the user.
